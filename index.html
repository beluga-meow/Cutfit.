Terms of use

  • Your photo stays with you. It is processed in your browser and is not uploaded or saved by this app.
  • Estimates only. Smart face landmarks help place measurement dots, but face-shape results are estimates. Review and adjust the dots yourself.
  • Barber details are a snapshot. Ratings, hours and prices came from Google Maps on Nov 21, 2026 and may have changed. Shops are listed for convenience, not endorsed.
  • Your reviews and saved looks stay local. Reviews and favorite hairstyles are stored in this browser on this device only. Clearing browser data may erase them.
  • Permissions. Your browser asks before using the camera. You can decline and upload a photo instead.
  • Use your own photos. Only use photos of yourself or of people who agreed.
  • Try-on is a concept preview. The hairstyle overlay is illustrative, not a photorealistic AI hair replacement. Side and back cards are generic references, not views of you.
  • Just for fun. The roulette and the guide chat are automated and are not recommendations.

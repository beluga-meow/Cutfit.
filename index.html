Cutfit guide

Terms of use

  • Photo processing. Face scanning and the 3D viewer run in your browser. If you choose Generate AI, the single photo you selected and a hair mask are sent to the CutFit AI service and fal.ai to create the hairstyle preview. Do not upload someone else’s photo without permission.
  • Estimates only. Face landmarks and the hair segmenter help measure your face, but face-shape results are estimates. Confidence is shown as High, Medium or Low, and you can fine-tune the points yourself.
  • Barber details are a snapshot. Ratings, hours and prices came from Google Maps on Nov 21, 2026 and may have changed. Shops are listed for convenience, not endorsed. Prices are in pesos.
  • Your reviews, scans and saved looks stay local. They are stored in this browser on this device. Clearing browser data may erase them, and “Delete my photos” removes them on demand.
  • AI results are temporary. Generated images are stored for a short time on our worker so you can view and download them, then deleted automatically.
  • Permissions. Your browser asks before using the camera, and we show a photo notice first. You can decline and upload a photo instead.
  • Use your own photos. Only use photos of yourself or of people who agreed.
  • Credits. AI credits are limited per device and per network address on the server. Abuse protection may block repeated requests.
  • AI try-on and references. AI results are generated estimates and may not perfectly match a real haircut. Side and back buttons show reference searches unless you add a side photo.
  • Just for fun. The roulette and the guide chat are automated and are not recommendations.

Your face data

Photos and face measurements can count as biometric data. Cutfit scans in your browser. Nothing is uploaded until you press Generate AI, and then only the single photo you chose is sent to our worker and fal.ai to make the preview. Other photos stay on this device.

  • Face scanning and the 3D viewer run entirely in your browser.
  • AI previews send one photo + a hair mask to our worker and fal.ai, then the result is stored for a short time and deleted automatically.
  • We do not create an account, and we do not sell data.
  • You can delete every photo Cutfit holds with one button.

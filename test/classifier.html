4. How to read it

  • Fit is a Gaussian-weighted agreement between the measurement and the prototype, not a probability.
  • Confidence combines fit, the margin over the runner-up, scan quality and frame stability. The app shows High/Medium/Low instead of a percentage.
  • Margin under 0.20 means two shapes are close — the app deliberately drops to Medium.
